# zennia

A web app for learning Japanese from zero: kana, then basic vocabulary. The name joins _zen_ and _omnia_.

## Register

product

## Users

Absolute beginners who study Japanese on their own and don't know where to start. Learn gives them a short lesson to read first, and each lesson fills their practice with what it taught, so they never have to build a word list before they can begin.

Learners who already study from a textbook or a course can bring their own vocabulary and practice it alongside, or instead of, the lessons' lists. They are served, but they are not the focus.

## Product Purpose

**zennia** takes a beginner from zero to reading hiragana, katakana and the basic (JLPT N5) vocabulary. It works in two steps:

- **Learn** explains the basics in short lessons, with every example heard on a click, and ends each one by adding its kana or words to practice.
- **Practice** shows a kana or a word, the user types its reading in romaji, and the app checks the answer by sound. Items the user misses come up more often. There is one Practice for everything: what the lessons added and what the user added themselves.

It grows in phases, and each one starts only once the previous one is shown to work with real learners:

1. **Practice** (done): the user adds their own words and practices them, along with the kana they choose.
2. **Learn**: short introductory lessons on hiragana, katakana and the N5 vocabulary, each ending by adding its list to practice.
3. **Basic grammar**: more Learn lessons, teaching grammar with the vocabulary the user already knows.
4. **Kanji**: the kanji of the words the user already knows, built from their components, with their readings from the start.

The order of phases 3 and 4 is decided once phase 2 shows where learners get stuck.

**zennia** doesn't teach kanji as a subject of its own before phase 4, nor conversation or listening beyond the pronunciation of each item.

## Brand Personality / Tone

- **Calm.** Unhurried and quiet, with no visual noise.
- **Welcoming.** Gentle with mistakes and encouraging.
- **Authentically Japanese.** It follows Japanese conventions over Western ones, such as the maru (○) for a correct answer.

The voice is friendly and light: conversational, with some warmth.

## Offer

**zennia** is free. Earning money from it later is welcome, but running costs stay near zero.
